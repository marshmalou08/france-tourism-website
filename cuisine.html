French Cuisine

A culinary tradition recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

The Art of French Cooking

French cuisine is celebrated around the world as one of the greatest culinary traditions in human history. It forms the foundation of classical cooking techniques that are taught in culinary schools globally. From the elegant simplicity of a perfectly roasted chicken to the technical mastery of a soufflé, French cooking elevates everyday ingredients into extraordinary experiences.

Each region of France contributes its own distinct dishes and ingredients to the national table — from the butter-rich sauces of Normandy, to the olive oil and herb-scented dishes of Provence, to the hearty stews of Burgundy and the charcuterie of the Auvergne. To eat in France is to travel through its landscapes, history, and culture in every bite.

Iconic French Dishes

Bouillabaisse

A traditional Provençal fish stew originating from Marseille, made with a rich saffron broth, fresh Mediterranean seafood, and served with crusty bread and rouille sauce. The soul of southern French coastal cooking.

Bœuf Bourguignon

A slow-braised beef stew from Burgundy, cooked with red wine, lardons, mushrooms, and pearl onions until the meat melts from the bone. Rich, deeply flavored, and a true classic of French home cooking.

Ratatouille

A vibrant vegetable stew from Provence made with tomatoes, zucchini, eggplant, peppers, and herbs. Bursting with the flavors of the Mediterranean summer, it is equally delicious served hot or cold.

Coq au Vin

Chicken slowly braised in red wine with mushrooms, lardons, and aromatic herbs until tender and flavorful. One of the most beloved dishes of French rural cuisine, perfected over generations.

Vichyssoise

A silky smooth cold soup made from puréed leeks, potatoes, cream, and chicken broth, garnished with chives. Elegant, refined, and a perfect example of French culinary sophistication.

Cassoulet

A slow-cooked casserole from the Languedoc region, combining white beans with various meats — typically duck confit, pork sausages, and sometimes lamb — in a rich, deeply savory sauce.
