PARIS

Paris, the iconic capital of France, stands as one of Europe's most vibrant and influential cities. Renowned worldwide as a hub of art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture, Paris seamlessly blends historic charm with modern sophistication. Stroll along its broad, tree-lined boulevards and the scenic River Seine, where world-famous landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and the Gothic masterpiece of Notre-Dame Cathedral await. Beyond its architectural wonders, the city captivates visitors with its renowned café culture, exquisite patisseries, and high-end designer boutiques along the elegant Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré.

Paris Gastronomy

Crêpe

A crêpe is a dish made from unleavened batter cooked on a griddle. They are often served with a wide variety of fillings such as cheese, fruit, vegetables, meats, and a variety of sweet or savory spreads.

Duck Confit

Duck confit is a classic French dish slow-cooked in its own fat, resulting in incredibly tender meat with a golden, crispy skin. A beloved staple of French gastronomy, especially in the Gascony region.

Escargot

Escargot are edible land snails served as a French delicacy, commonly prepared with garlic butter, parsley, and white wine. They are considered a high-protein delicacy with a tender, meaty texture.

Croque Monsieur

Croque Monsieur is a delicious French ham and cheese sandwich made with gruyère, parmesan, ham, and a béchamel sauce, toasted in the oven until golden and bubbly.
