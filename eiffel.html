Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is a 330-meter (1,083 ft) wrought-iron lattice tower in Paris, designed by Gustave Eiffel for the Exposition Universelle of 1889. Known as the "Iron Lady," it has become a global symbol of France and a must-visit attraction for travelers worldwide.

The tower features three visitor levels, including restaurants, observation decks, and exhibits about its history. From the top, guests can enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Paris, spotting landmarks like the Louvre Museum and Arc de Triomphe. Beyond tourism, it also functions as a broadcasting tower, transmitting radio and TV signals across the city.

At night, the Eiffel Tower sparkles with thousands of lights, creating a magical experience for visitors and locals alike. Today, it remains one of the most visited and celebrated monuments in the world, combining history, architecture, and stunning views in one unforgettable destination.
