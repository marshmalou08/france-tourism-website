Destination Spotlight

Paris, France

Welcome to the mesmerizing realm of Paris — a city where every cobblestone holds whispers of history and every avenue unfurls a tapestry of experiences. From the bohemian spirit of Montmartre to the timeless elegance of the Latin Quarter, Paris is a kaleidoscope of emotions waiting to be explored. Every corner is a masterpiece, and every bite is an ode to gastronomic delight.

Fun Facts About France

  • France is consistently among the most visited countries in the world.
  • There are over 1,600 types of cheese produced in France.
  • The Louvre is the largest art museum on Earth.
  • The Eiffel Tower was originally intended to be temporary and was almost demolished in 1909.
  • French cuisine is recognized as a UNESCO intangible cultural heritage.
  • France has the most time zones of any country in the world — 12 in total.
